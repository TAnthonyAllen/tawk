Bridging the gap is the Phase 2 work.

- [ ] **Phase C** — first clean compile of new-runtime TAWK
- [ ] **Phase D** — Tests/ sandbox verification vs `~/bin/tok`

- `~/bin/tok` is the safety net — broken TAWK can't fix itself
